# Product Vision

## Thesis

LAGGENTE is the agentic operating system for real estate.

It begins by giving every human real-estate agent a living digital branch office: an AI presence that knows the professional's territory, services, methods, boundaries, availability, knowledge, and style. It can listen, explain, qualify, collect evidence, schedule, summarize, and bring the human into the relationship at the correct moment.

The first commercially valuable job is precise:

> A homeowner opens an agent's personal link. LAGGENTE conducts the first conversation, qualifies the opportunity, moves it toward a valuation, and delivers an actionable dossier to the human agent.

## Why this is not a chatbot

“Customizable chatbot” describes an interface component. LAGGENTE is an operating loop:

> conversation → qualification → appointment → dossier → human takeover

The product succeeds when agents repeatedly send real prospects to it because it creates useful opportunities. Engagement without commercial movement is not the objective.

## The human and the machine

AI owns continuity, availability, structured memory, and repeatable execution. The human professional owns judgment, responsibility, negotiation, physical presence, and local trust.

LAGGENTE must never impersonate accountability. The customer must always know whether the current speaker is the AI assistant or the named human professional.

## Expansion path

1. Seller acquisition engine.
2. Full digital front office across seller, buyer, rental, visits, follow-up, voice, calendar, WhatsApp, and CRM connections.
3. A structured network of augmented real-estate agents.
4. Transaction orchestration across documents, technicians, finance, insurance, legal steps, approvals, and evidence.
5. A real-estate interface in which a person expresses an intention and the correct process and network assemble around it.

## Product principles

- Every serious prospect deserves an immediate intelligent response.
- Every professional deserves protection from unqualified noise.
- Conversation is the natural interface, but structured, versioned state is the foundation.
- AI should multiply excellent agents rather than conceal or replace them.
- The personal subdomain is a node in the future network, not merely a landing page.
- Information is abundant; responsibility, judgment, and physical presence remain scarce.
- One person equipped with agents can act like an organization.
- The model is not the moat. The moat is learning which conversations create trust and commercial outcomes.

## North Star

> Qualified valuation appointments per agent per week.
