# MVP Scope

## Pilot

- First professional: Mauro.
- Initial cohort: five real-estate agents.
- Commercial wedge: seller acquisition.
- First Playbook: understand a prospective seller and move the person toward a professional valuation or human handoff.
- Initial commercial model: free pilot.

## Required professional experience

1. Mauro signs in through a magic link.
2. LAGGENTE Studio interviews him conversationally about territory, services, tone, required information, objections, boundaries, availability, and handoff rules.
3. The Builder converts the conversation into validated draft configuration.
4. Mauro sees a structured interpretation and a concrete preview.
5. He follows Draft → Preview → Approve → Publish.
6. The published change becomes active without a code deployment.
7. Mauro operates opportunities from an inbox ordered by next action.
8. He can inspect the dossier and transcript and take over the same conversation.

## Required homeowner experience

1. The homeowner opens `mauro.laggente.com` without creating an account.
2. LAGGENTE clearly identifies itself as Mauro's AI assistant in the first message.
3. The person can use text, a voice note, and a limited number of property photographs.
4. The Seller Playbook collects facts progressively, one meaningful question at a time.
5. Answers use only published professional information and approved platform content.
6. The homeowner sees and can correct an editable summary.
7. A confirmed summary creates an actionable opportunity dossier.
8. “Talk to Mauro” remains available.
9. Human takeover is announced and visually unmistakable; automatic replies pause.

## Seller facts

- intention and motivation;
- relationship to the property and co-ownership;
- address or area;
- property type and essential characteristics;
- condition and occupancy;
- desired timing;
- price expectation when volunteered;
- name and usable contact details;
- preferred contact method;
- valuation request or agreed next action.

## Included

- multi-tenant accounts and members;
- versioned professional configuration;
- one Seller Playbook;
- private Builder and public Concierge;
- streaming text chat;
- chained voice-note transcription with an editable transcript;
- private photograph uploads;
- approved knowledge;
- editable lead summary;
- opportunity dashboard and dossier;
- email notification;
- human handoff and takeover state;
- AI disclosure, consent events, retention controls, and audit events;
- personal wildcard subdomains.

## Explicitly excluded from the first slice

- billing and public self-service signup;
- native WhatsApp bot;
- property portal feeds and property search;
- automated valuation presented as certainty;
- renovation rendering;
- full CRM;
- transaction document orchestration;
- agent-to-agent network;
- property passports, blockchain proofs, crypto, payments, or title transfer;
- multi-agent swarms;
- infrastructure per professional subdomain;
- customer-provided OpenAI keys.

## Definition of done

The first vertical slice is complete when:

1. Mauro signs in.
2. He changes at least one required question and the tone through conversation.
3. He sees the structured diff, approves it, and publishes.
4. The change appears on `mauro.laggente.com` without redeployment.
5. A visitor communicates through text or audio.
6. The AI identifies itself correctly.
7. The visitor confirms an editable summary.
8. The opportunity appears in Mauro's dashboard.
9. Mauro receives a notification.
10. Mauro takes over and the visitor clearly sees that a human is speaking.
